🧪 Lab & Experiment Desk

Saare lab upkaran, ek hi section me
Experiment ki shuruaat se paper tak ka har kaam isi ek desk par. Ye upkaran chat ke dwara chalte hain — Babu ko bolo, kaam hokar yahi milega (dusre charan me inke apne form aayenge).
💊 Dose Guru
Purani khoj + LD50 se surakshit dose ki range, safety margin, aur kitne janwar (n) chahiye — 3Rs ke saath.
🧫 Fingerprint Helper
HPTLC/HPLC se extract ki pehchan, batch-to-batch tulna, aur journal ke layak fingerprint report.
🗄️ Fail Vault
Fail hue prayog aur na-kaam doses ka record — naya design bante hi purani galti par chetaavni.
📓 Live Lab Diary
Roz ki readings jama hoti jayein, graph khud bante jayein, ajeeb number (outlier) par turant sanket.
📊 Statistics Doctor
Sahi test khud chune — ANOVA/t-test, Shapiro-Levene jaanch, exact P aur effect size (η²) ke saath.
📈 Figure Scientist
Data se journal-standard graph — sahi axis, sahi unit, significance ke nishaan; galat unit bane hi na.
🔗 Ye desk IAEC Protocol (Teen Roop) aur Quality Gate se juda hai — lab ka data seedha paper me jayega.

🛡️ Humanizer + Originality Shield

Har paper isse guzarta hai
✍️ 1. Paragraph Humanizer
Har paragraph likhte samay hi insaani lehje me — vaaky lambai-chhotai badalti hui, AI wale ghise-pite shabd hataye hue.
🔍 2. Nakal-Pakad (Similarity Self-Check)
Draft ko uske apne sources se milaya jata hai — source ke bahut paas wala hissa dobara apne shabdon me likha jata hai, sahi citation ke saath.
🤖 3. AI-Pattern Report
Robotic pattern, dohraaye shabd aur ek-jaise vaaky — Quality Gate par puri report.
⚖️ Factory ka sachcha usool: ye teenon nakal aur robotic lekhan ko asal me bahut kam kar dete hain — lekin “100% detector-pass” ki guarantee duniya ka koi asal tool nahi de sakta. Asal suraksha hai: nakal bilkul nahi + har daave ka sahi citation.
